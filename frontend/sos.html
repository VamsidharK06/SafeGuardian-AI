How this works

Pressing SOS gets your current GPS location, tries to capture a short camera image and a short voice clip (best-effort — SOS still proceeds if either isn't available), and sends everything to the backend. The backend runs a context-based AI assessment, looks up every saved contact, and sends each of them the emergency message, current location, and any captured evidence through Twilio's WhatsApp Business API — automatically, with no browser tabs and no manual "tap Send" step. If WhatsApp isn't configured on the server yet, or a specific contact or evidence item can't be delivered, that's reported honestly rather than claimed as sent.
